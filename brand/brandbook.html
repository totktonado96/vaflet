One light

The idea

The logo is two objects on a sheet of paper, lit by the same lamp. The sphere is drawn only with engraving lines. The word casts a block shadow, hatched like an etching.

Black ink, white paper, and the light between them. Everything else in the identity comes from that: hatching instead of grey, stripes instead of gradients, and shadows that always fall the same way.

Construction

Proportions and clear space
  • The mark box is 48 units. The word is 36 units tall including its shadow and sits centred on the mark.
  • The gap between mark and word is 8 units. Do not tighten or widen it.
  • Clear space on every side equals the gap between the sphere and the word, x. Nothing enters it.
  • Shadows fall down and to the right at rest (54° below the horizon). Every shadow in a layout falls the same way.

Hatch and motion

How it moves
  • Hatching runs at 45°, rising to the right. The stripe equals the gap.
  • Large surfaces use the ink-field patterns from the site: flowing zebra stripes, ripples, topographic lines. Always pure black and white, never blurred.
  • The logo's light drifts about ±30° on an 18-second swing. On hover it follows the pointer, then eases back.
  • Respect reduced motion: the logo then rests at the default light.

Voice

How we sound

Plain, confident, a little dry. We say what we build and what happens next, then we stop.

The founders lead every project. We never state a headcount and never describe ourselves by size. The client decides how big we are from the work.

  • "A founder on the first call and a founder at launch."
  • "We read every word and answer the same day."
  • "A two-person studio", "just the two of us", "no account managers because we don't have any".
  • "Vaflet LLC" in headlines. It belongs in the footer and contracts.
